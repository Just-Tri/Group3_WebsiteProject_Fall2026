1958 — Tennis for Two

Physicist William Higinbotham creates an early interactive game on an oscilloscope. It is one of the first games made for entertainment.

1972 — The Arcade Begins

Atari releases Pong. Its simple table-tennis gameplay becomes a huge success and helps introduce video games to the public.

Late 1970s — Home Consoles

The Atari 2600 brings interchangeable game cartridges into living rooms, making home gaming more popular.

1980s — Arcade Icons

Games such as Pac-Man, Donkey Kong, and Tetris became cultural icons. Nintendo also introduces the Nintendo Entertainment System (NES) which was famously Launched with Super Mario Bros and Duck Hunt.

1990s — 3D Gaming

Consoles like the PlayStation and Nintendo 64 make 3D worlds widely available. Games become larger, more cinematic, and more immersive.

2000s — Gaming Goes Online

Broadband internet allows players to compete and cooperate online. Xbox Live, PC gaming, and massive multiplayer games connect people around the world.

2010s — Mobile and Indie Games

Smartphones put games in millions of pockets, while independent creators reach new audiences through digital stores and streaming platforms.

2020s — Games Today

Games are played across consoles, computers, phones, and cloud services. Cross-play, virtual reality, live updates, and esports continue to change how people play.
